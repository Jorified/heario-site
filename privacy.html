Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Who we are

Heario ("we", "us") is operated by [YOUR LEGAL NAME], trading as Heario, a sole trader, [BUSINESS ADDRESS], Ireland. Contact: support@heario.ai.

Heario provides an AI chat assistant that businesses ("Customers") add to their websites. It answers visitors' questions and notifies the Customer of enquiries by WhatsApp and email.

We act in two roles:

  • Controller for the personal data of people who visit heario.ai and of Customers' account and billing details (sections 3 and 4).
  • Processor for chat conversations and enquiries collected through a Customer's website. The Customer is the controller of that data, and our Data Processing Agreement applies (section 5).

2. If you chat with a Heario assistant on another business's website

The business decides why your data is collected and is responsible for it, so please read that business's own privacy notice. The chat window tells you that you are talking to an AI assistant. If you give your name and contact details, they are passed to that business. Heario processes your messages only on that business's behalf.

What we process: the messages you type, and any details you volunteer (such as your name, phone number or email). Your conversation is held in your browser's session storage so it survives page changes, and is cleared when you close the tab. We do not store the full conversation on our servers. We store only the enquiry (name, contact details and a short summary) that the assistant sends to the business, and we keep your IP address briefly for abuse prevention (see section 4). If the assistant can’t answer a question about the business, we also save that question so the business can add an answer. The assistant is told to word it generally and leave out names and contact details, and we automatically remove any email addresses and phone numbers. If you leave an email address, the business may have us send you one email confirming that your enquiry was received; replies to it go to the business. Please don't share sensitive information such as card numbers, passwords or health details in the chat.

2A. If you chat with the assistant on heario.ai

The chat on our own website is Heario's assistant, running on Heario. Here we are the controller. The assistant answers your questions about Heario. If you choose to leave your name, contact details and a question, it sends them to us by WhatsApp or email so we can reply, and saves them as an enquiry. As with any Heario chat, your conversation is kept only in your browser's session storage; we keep the enquiry, not the full conversation. We use your details only to reply to you, never for marketing lists, and enquiries are deleted automatically after 90 days. Your messages are processed by Anthropic to generate replies (see section 6).

4. How long we keep it

  • Account and chatbot settings: until you delete your account from the dashboard. Deletion removes your account, chatbot, enquiries and saved questions from our systems immediately.
  • Enquiries: deleted automatically after 90 days, or sooner if you delete your account.
  • Questions the assistant couldn’t answer: deleted automatically 90 days after they were last asked, or sooner if you delete your account.
  • Chat usage counters: up to 13 months.
  • Login tokens: deleted shortly after they expire.
  • IP addresses used for rate limiting: up to 2 days.
  • Billing and tax records (held by Stripe and us): up to 6 years, as Irish tax law requires.

5. Chat data we process for Customers (as processor)

We use visitors' messages only to generate replies, notify the Customer of enquiries, and operate and secure the service. We do not use chat content to train AI models. Details are in the Data Processing Agreement.

6. Who we share data with (sub-processors)

  • Anthropic: the AI model that generates replies (United States)
  • Meta Platforms Ireland (WhatsApp Business Platform): delivers WhatsApp alerts
  • Vercel: website and application hosting
  • Neon: database hosting
  • Brevo: sends login and enquiry emails, and enquiry confirmations to visitors
  • Stripe: payments

Each has its own privacy practices. We share only what each needs to provide its service.

7. Transfers outside the EEA

Some of these providers process data in the United States. Where they do, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified providers, or on EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your personal data, and object to processing. Where we act as the controller, email support@heario.ai. If your data is in a chat on a Customer's website, we will pass your request to that Customer, who is responsible for it. You can complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

9. Cookies and local storage

We use one strictly necessary cookie to keep you logged in to the dashboard. The chat widget uses your browser's session storage to keep the current conversation while you browse, and to remember if you've closed its greeting bubble; it is not a tracking cookie. We do not use advertising cookies.
