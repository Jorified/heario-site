Data Processing Agreement

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Terms & Conditions between the Customer ("Controller") and [YOUR LEGAL NAME], trading as Heario, a sole trader, [BUSINESS ADDRESS], Ireland ("Processor"), and applies automatically when the Customer creates a Heario account. It meets the requirements of Article 28 GDPR.

1. Scope

Processor provides an AI website chat assistant with WhatsApp and email enquiry alerts (the "Service"). In doing so Processor processes personal data on Controller's behalf.

2. Details of processing

  • Subject matter: operating the chat assistant on Controller's websites and notifying Controller of enquiries.
  • Duration: the term of the Service, plus the deletion periods in clause 9.
  • Nature and purpose: receiving visitors' messages, generating replies with an AI model, extracting enquiry details, sending alerts to Controller, and showing enquiries in Controller's dashboard.
  • Types of personal data: names, phone numbers, email addresses, IP addresses (briefly, for rate limiting) and any other information visitors type into the chat.
  • Data subjects: visitors to Controller's websites.
  • Special categories: the Service is not designed for them. Controller must not configure the assistant to request them.

3. Processor's obligations

Processor will:

  1. process personal data only on Controller's documented instructions (this DPA and Controller's use of the dashboard), unless EU or Irish law requires otherwise, in which case Processor will tell Controller first where lawful;
  2. ensure anyone authorised to process the data is bound by confidentiality;
  3. implement the security measures in Annex 2;
  4. respect the sub-processor conditions in clause 5;
  5. assist Controller, by appropriate technical and organisational measures, in responding to data subjects' requests;
  6. assist Controller with security, breach notification, impact assessments and prior consultation, taking into account the nature of processing;
  7. delete or return personal data at the end of the Service (clause 9);
  8. make available the information needed to show compliance, and allow audits (clause 8);
  9. tell Controller if an instruction appears to infringe data protection law.

Processor does not use chat content to train AI models and does not sell personal data.

4. Controller's obligations

Controller confirms it has a lawful basis for the processing, has given visitors the required privacy information (including that they are chatting with an AI and that the details they share are passed to Controller), and has the agreement of the person who will receive WhatsApp alerts. Controller will not use the Service to collect special-category data or children's data.

5. Sub-processors

Controller authorises the sub-processors in Annex 1. Processor will give at least 14 days' notice of additions or replacements by email or dashboard notice. Controller may object on reasonable data protection grounds; if the objection can't be resolved, Controller may cancel and receive a pro-rata refund of any prepaid period. Processor imposes equivalent data protection obligations on each sub-processor and remains responsible for their performance.

6. International transfers

Where processing involves a transfer outside the EEA, Processor ensures an appropriate safeguard under Chapter V GDPR: an adequacy decision or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where applicable, or Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Personal data breaches

Processor will notify Controller without undue delay, and within 48 hours of becoming aware, of a breach affecting Controller's personal data, with the information then reasonably available (nature, categories and approximate numbers affected, likely consequences, and measures taken).

8. Audits

On reasonable notice, and no more than once a year unless a breach has occurred, Processor will provide the information needed to show compliance and allow audits by Controller or its auditor, subject to confidentiality and without unreasonable disruption.

9. Return and deletion

Controller can delete its account and all its data at any time from the dashboard, which erases it immediately. Enquiries are otherwise deleted automatically after 90 days. On termination, personal data is deleted as above unless the law requires its retention.

10. Liability and governing law

Liability is as set out in the Terms & Conditions. This DPA is governed by the laws of Ireland and the courts of Ireland have jurisdiction.

Annex 2: Security measures

  • HTTPS/TLS for all traffic; encryption at rest provided by hosting and database providers.
  • Secrets held in server-side environment variables, never in browser code.
  • Per-customer chatbot keys, restricted in browsers to the websites Controller lists.
  • Per-visitor and per-chatbot rate limits, and message limits, to limit abuse.
  • Passwordless login with single-use, expiring, hashed tokens; signed HttpOnly session cookies.
  • Full chat transcripts are not stored; enquiries are kept 90 days; deletion on request.
  • Breach response procedure, and routine dependency updates.
