4. Add it to your website

It takes about 5 minutes. You copy one line of code and paste it into your website builder's settings, once, and the chat appears on every page.

  1. Check your website address is in section 3.
  2. Copy your code.
    Email it to my web developer
  3. Paste it into your website builder. Pick yours for step-by-step help:
  4. Check it's working. Open your website in a private or incognito window. The chat bubble should appear in the bottom corner within a few seconds. It won't show inside your builder's editor or preview, only on your published site.
    Bubble not showing?
    • Make sure you published or saved the change in your builder.
    • Check the exact address you're visiting (for example yoursite.webflow.io or yourshop.myshopify.com) is listed in section 3, then click Save.
    • Check "Chatbot is switched on" is ticked in section 3.
    • Paste the code only once. If you've also added it to a page block, remove that copy.
    • Still stuck? Email support@heario.ai with your website address and we'll help.
