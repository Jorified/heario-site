Getting started

What is Heario?

Heario adds an AI assistant to your website. It answers visitors' questions 24/7 using information about your business, and when someone wants a quote, a booking or a callback, it takes their details and sends them straight to your WhatsApp.

How long does setup take?

About 10 minutes. You tell it about your business, add your WhatsApp number, and paste one line of code onto your website. Your dashboard walks you through each step.

Do I have to type in all my business information?

No. Enter your website address and Heario reads your pages and writes a draft of your services, prices, opening hours and common questions. You check it, change anything that's wrong and save it. Nothing goes live until you do. You can also type or paste it in yourself.

Which websites does it work on?

Any website where you can add a line of code before the closing body tag. That includes WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, Webflow, Framer, Square Online and custom-built sites. See our step-by-step install guides.

Do I need a web developer?

No. Most website builders have a setting where you paste the code once, and our install guides show exactly where. If you do have a web developer, you can email them your code and instructions straight from your dashboard.

Can you help me set it up?

Yes. During your free trial, email support@heario.ai with your website address and we'll help you get it set up, free. If you'd rather we did it with you, you can add the optional one-off setup service when you subscribe.

Will it slow down my website?

No. The chat loads after the rest of your page, so it doesn't hold up your page appearing for visitors.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. The chat works on phones, tablets and computers, and resizes to fit the screen.

How the AI works

What AI does it use?

Replies are generated by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic. Visitors are told they're chatting with an AI assistant.

Will it make things up?

It's instructed to answer only from the information you provide and never to invent prices or promises. If it doesn't know, it says so and offers to pass the question to you. Keep your business information accurate and up to date for the best results.

What happens when it can't answer a question?

It offers to take the visitor's details and pass the question to you. The question is also saved under "Questions it couldn't answer" in your dashboard, so you can add the answer and it will know it next time.

Can it reply in other languages?

Yes. It replies in the visitor's language, even if your business information is written in English.

Does it know my opening hours?

You can add your opening hours and holiday closures in your dashboard. The assistant uses them to tell visitors when you'll get back to them.

Can I change how the chat looks and greets visitors?

Yes. You can choose the chat colour, a light or dark style, and how it says hello: a personal greeting using your first name, a business greeting, or a small pop-up message that invites visitors to ask a question.

Enquiries and WhatsApp

How do enquiries reach me?

When a visitor leaves their name, contact details and what they need, you get a WhatsApp message from Heario's number straight away. Every enquiry is also saved in your dashboard. If a visitor says it's an emergency or they need help today, the alert is marked urgent.

Do I need WhatsApp Business?

No. Alerts arrive in your normal WhatsApp from Heario's number. You confirm you're happy to receive them when you set up, and you can switch them off at any time.

What if I don't use WhatsApp?

Enquiries are emailed to you instead. If a WhatsApp alert ever fails, it's emailed to you as a backup. You can also choose to get an email copy of every enquiry.

Can visitors get a confirmation?

Yes, if you switch it on. Visitors who leave an email address get a short confirmation in your business's name, and if they reply, it comes to your email.

Pricing and the free trial

How much does it cost?

It's free for 14 days, then €19.99 a month (VAT included), or £16.99 or $22.99 if you're in the UK or US. There's no contract and you can cancel any time.

Do I need a card for the free trial?

No. You only enter card details if you decide to subscribe.

What happens after the free trial?

You choose whether to subscribe. If you don't, the chat stops answering and visitors are told to contact you directly. Nothing is charged automatically, because we don't take a card for the trial.

Is there a limit on messages?

The plan includes up to 3,000 chat messages a month, which is plenty for most small businesses. We email you at 80% and if you reach the limit. If you do, AI replies pause until the next month, but visitors can still send you enquiries with a short form, so you won't miss any. Contact us if you need more.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from your dashboard. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. If you're not happy within 7 days of your first payment, we'll refund it in full. See our refund policy.

Privacy and data

Do you store my visitors' chat conversations?

No. The conversation is kept only in the visitor's own browser for that visit. We keep the enquiry they choose to send you, and delete enquiries automatically after 90 days. Messages are processed by Anthropic to generate replies.

What about GDPR?

You're the data controller for your visitors' enquiries and Heario is your processor. Our data processing agreement and privacy policy explain exactly what's processed, by whom, and for how long. You should also mention the chat in your own privacy notice.
