FAQ

Questions

What AI does it use?

Replies are generated by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic. Visitors are told they're chatting with an AI assistant.

Will it make things up?

It's instructed to answer only from the information you provide and never to invent prices or promises. If it doesn't know, it says so and offers to pass the question to you. Keep your business information accurate and up to date for the best results.

Do I need WhatsApp Business?

No. Alerts arrive in your normal WhatsApp from Heario's number. You confirm you're happy to receive them when you set up, and you can switch them off at any time.

What happens after the free trial?

You choose whether to subscribe. If you don't, the chat bubble stops answering and visitors are told to contact you directly. Nothing is charged automatically because we don't take a card for the trial.

Which websites does it work on?

Any site where you can add a line of code before the closing body tag, including WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify and custom sites. Browsers will only run it on the websites you list in your dashboard, and usage limits protect you from misuse.

What about my visitors' data and GDPR?

You're the data controller for your visitors' enquiries and Heario is your processor. Our data processing agreement and privacy policy explain exactly what's processed, by whom, and for how long. You should also mention the chat in your own privacy notice.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from your dashboard. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.
