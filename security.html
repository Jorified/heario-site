Security

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Our approach

Heario handles business enquiries, so we keep the data we hold small and protect it carefully. We don't store full chat transcripts on our servers. We keep the enquiries your assistant sends you for 90 days, and account settings until you delete your account.

What we do

  • Encryption in transit: all traffic uses HTTPS (TLS), with HSTS enabled.
  • Passwordless login: one-time login links that expire after 15 minutes and work only once. The tokens are stored hashed. There are no passwords to leak.
  • Sessions: signed, HttpOnly, Secure cookies. State-changing requests are checked to come from our own site.
  • Restricted to your websites: browsers will only run your chatbot on the website addresses you list. Because the chatbot key is visible in your page source, this does not stop a determined person using a script, so we add the limits below.
  • Abuse limits: per-visitor rate limits, an hourly cap on messages and on enquiries for each chatbot, message length limits, and a monthly message cap limit spam and runaway costs.
  • Safe rendering: chat replies are shown as plain text, never as HTML, and the assistant is instructed to ignore attempts by visitors to change its rules.
  • Secrets: API keys are held in server-side environment variables and never sent to browsers.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 provider. Heario never sees or stores card details.
  • Erasure: you can delete your account and all data from the dashboard at any time.

Sub-processors

We use Anthropic (AI replies), Meta/WhatsApp (alerts), Vercel (hosting), Neon (database), Brevo (email) and Stripe (payments). See our Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement.

Responsible disclosure

If you find a security vulnerability, please report it privately before disclosing it publicly. Email support@heario.ai with the subject "Security Disclosure". We will acknowledge your report within 48 hours and work with you to fix the issue.
