Refund Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Free trial

Every new account starts with a 14-day free trial. We don't take payment details for the trial, so there is nothing to refund and nothing is charged automatically.

7-day money-back guarantee

If you subscribe and are not satisfied, contact us within 7 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full, no questions asked.

Optional setup assistance fee

The optional one-off setup fee covers personal help getting your chatbot set up. If you ask for a refund within 7 days of paying it and we have not yet started the setup work, we will refund it in full. Once setup work has started, the fee is not refundable. Your subscription is covered separately by the 7-day money-back guarantee above.

Monthly subscriptions

You can cancel at any time from your dashboard (Manage billing). Cancelling stops future charges; the current paid period is not refunded and you keep access until it ends, except under the guarantee above.

Not refundable

  • Requests made more than 7 days after your first payment, or for renewal payments
  • Accounts that breach our Terms & Conditions

How to request a refund

Email support@heario.ai with the subject "Refund Request" and the email address on your account. We process eligible refunds within 5 business days; funds typically reach you within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank.
