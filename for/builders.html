How it works

  1. Tell it about your business. Enter your website address and Heario writes a draft of your services, prices and opening hours for you to check, or type it in yourself.
  2. Add your WhatsApp number. Enquiries arrive in your normal WhatsApp. You don't need WhatsApp Business.
  3. Paste one line of code onto your website. Our install guides cover WordPress, Wix, Squarespace and more. It takes about 10 minutes in total.

Common questions

Will it make things up?

It's instructed to answer only from the information you provide and never to invent prices or promises. If it doesn't know, it says so and offers to pass the question to you. Keep your business information accurate and up to date for the best results.

Do I need WhatsApp Business?

No. Alerts arrive in your normal WhatsApp from Heario's number. You confirm you're happy to receive them when you set up, and you can switch them off at any time.

How much does it cost?

It's free for 14 days, then €19.99 a month (VAT included), or £16.99 or $22.99 if you're in the UK or US. There's no contract and you can cancel any time.
